Mojang shut off the public name-history endpoint in 2022, so past names come from laby.net's archive of that data — accounts can opt out of it, and accounts that never changed their name will show a single entry.
Skin history is stitched together from crafty.gg and laby.net. Capes come from the session server (the one equipped now) plus capes.dev (LabyMod, OptiFine, MinecraftCapes and friends).
Requests fall back through public CORS proxies when a source blocks the browser directly. No key, no build step, no backend.
